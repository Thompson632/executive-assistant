# AI Workflows

## Switch Context

Ask Codex to save where you left off on an action in [[Action-Items]].
Review the current state and next step before saving. When you return, ask
Codex to pick up that action and tell you what to do first.

## Quick Notes

Ask Codex to use `$executive-quick-note` when you want to jot something
down. It uses your existing capture note or adds a dated bullet to
`notes/Quick Notes.md`.

## Daily Brief

Generate a reviewed daily report from vault signals. Include relevant metadata from configured local Outlook sources or explicitly supplied calendar or mail files.

## Weekly Review

Prepare the upcoming week, carry forward unresolved important items, and identify decisions or blocked work.

## Meeting Prep

Review agenda, related notes, open actions, and likely decisions before the meeting. Debrief afterward into actions and decisions.
