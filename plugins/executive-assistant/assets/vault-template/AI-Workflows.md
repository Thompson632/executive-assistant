# AI Workflows

## Switch Context

Ask Codex to save where you left off on an action in [[Action-Items]].
Review the current state and next step before saving. When you return, ask
Codex to pick up that action and tell you what to do first.

## Quick Notes

Ask Codex to use `$executive-quick-note` when you want to jot something
down. It uses your existing capture note or adds a dated bullet to
`notes/Quick Notes.md`.

## Daily Brief

Generate a reviewed daily report from vault signals. Include relevant metadata from configured local Outlook sources or explicitly supplied calendar or mail files.

## Weekly Review

Prepare the upcoming week, carry forward unresolved important items, and identify decisions or blocked work.

## Meeting Prep

Review the agenda, related notes, and open actions before the meeting.
Afterward, ask Codex to extract your actions and preview adding them to
[[Action-Items]]. Include due dates when known.

## One-on-Ones

Use `$executive-one-on-one` to summarize raw notes and save them by person
and date. Before the next meeting, ask for open commitments and questions
to raise. See [[One-on-Ones]] for prompts.

## Complete an Action

Check off the original task in [[Action-Items]] or ask Codex to preview
the change. Saved daily and weekly reports show the state at the time
they were generated. Follow their links for the current task status.
