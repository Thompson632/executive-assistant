# One-on-Ones

Open `meetings/One-on-Ones/` to find your notes by person and meeting date.
Use the [[templates/One-on-One|meeting template]] to start a note manually.

- **After a meeting:** “Use `$executive-one-on-one` to save these notes from
  my 1:1 with Alex on November 16, 2026. Summarize the decisions and preview
  adding my commitments to Actions.”
- **Before the next meeting:** “Use `$executive-one-on-one` to prep my next
  1:1 with Alex. Show open commitments, due dates, and what to discuss.”
